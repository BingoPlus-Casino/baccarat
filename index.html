01 / Fundamentals

How to Play Baccarat

Baccarat is a comparing-card game between two hands called Player and Banker. You are wagering on the result of those hands, not necessarily playing as either participant. The objective is to predict which hand finishes closest to nine, or whether the hands tie.

Select a wager: Player, Banker, or Tie.
Two cards are dealt to each hand. Tens and face cards count as zero; aces count as one.
Only the last digit of the total counts: a seven plus eight becomes five.
Additional cards, if required, are dealt automatically under fixed third-card rules.
The hand closer to nine wins; a matching total is a tie.

A two-card total of eight or nine is called a natural. In standard punto banco, the drawing process is not a player decision.

02 / Betting options

Understanding Baccarat Casino Bets

01

Banker

A bet that the Banker hand will win. Many conventional tables apply a commission to Banker wins; check the table's posted payout rules.

02

Player

A bet that the Player hand will win. The word “Player” describes the hand, not the identity of the person placing the wager.

03

Tie

A bet that both hands finish with the same total. Tie bets generally have a higher house edge than the two primary wagers.

Important: Baccarat is a chance-based game. Previous rounds do not reliably predict the next outcome, and no betting pattern guarantees a win.

05 / Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play baccarat?

Choose Player, Banker, or Tie before cards are dealt. The hand nearest nine wins under fixed drawing rules.

Is baccarat a game of skill?

Standard baccarat follows predetermined card-drawing rules. Players choose bets but do not control the cards.

What does a natural mean in baccarat?

An initial two-card total of eight or nine is a natural and usually ends the round without further cards.

Can I play baccarat online?

Digital and live-dealer versions may be available where lawful, subject to operator requirements and age restrictions.

Does Banker always win more often?

Banker has a small statistical advantage under common punto banco rules, but every individual round remains uncertain. Payout commissions can affect the effective return.

Can a betting system guarantee baccarat winnings?

No. Systems that change bet size do not remove the house edge or eliminate the possibility of losses.
